# 高潮与射精表现（视频向）

## 原则
- 高潮是「释放」而不是突然爆炸。先写身体信号，再写峰值，最后写余韵。
- 与动作、声音、表情严格同步。
- 男性与女性高潮表现不同，分开写更真实。

## 女性高潮常用表达
- 接近：`her breathing quickens, inner muscles tightening around him`
- 峰值：`her body tenses and trembles, back arching, a high soft moan escaping as she climaxes`
- 余韵：`she continues to tremble lightly, inner walls pulsing, soft aftershocks running through her`

## 男性高潮 / 射精
- 接近：`his thrusts become irregular, breathing heavy, muscles tensing`
- 内射：`he pushes deep and holds, releasing thick pulses of semen inside her`
- 外射：`he pulls out and cums, thick white ropes of semen landing across her skin / breasts / face`
- 余韵：`he stays pressed against her, breathing hard, residual pulses fading`

## 同步写法示例
```
As her moans rise in pitch and her body tightens, he thrusts deeper. She climaxes first, trembling and contracting around him. A few thrusts later he follows, pushing fully inside and releasing.
```

## 声音
高潮时享受声升高、变短促，结束后转为急促喘息。在 overall_soundscape 与动作处同步写明。
