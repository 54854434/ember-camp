# 男女生殖器精确写法（H3 专用）

基于社区实测（WeissGoat practice、xingheFF H3NSFW skill、Velvet Vice 等）总结的正向精确描述。

## 核心原则
- **正向重复关键事实**（Positive-Repeat）：插入、接触、正确形状至少在 summary、action、end state 出现 2–3 次。
- **绝对接触状态**：写 “fully inserted / pressed flush / correctly shaped”，禁止悬空、滑过腹部等模糊表述。
- **可见时才详细写**：下体清楚入画时才展开解剖描述。

## 女性生殖器
推荐英文：
- `accurately shaped natural female genitals with visible labia and dark pubic hair`
- `correct proportions, realistic anatomy without distortion or fusion`
- `her vagina`, `labia`, `clitoris`（需要时）
- 接触：`the hand makes clear contact with her natural female genitals matching the reference. The soft flesh responds naturally.`

插入时标准正向重复：
```
his erect penis is inserted in her vagina; he thrusts in and out of her vagina
```
在 summary、动作段、结束状态各写一次。

## 男性生殖器
- `correctly proportioned erect penis with natural shape and vein detail`
- `accurate glans and shaft proportions`
- `testicles` / `scrotum`（需要时）
- 避免过度夸张导致变形。

## 常见失败修复
- 异形/融合 → 加入 “without distortion, fusion or deformation” + 正向形状描述
- 比例崩 → “correct proportions”, “realistic anatomy”
- 接触不准 → 绝对状态 + 重复

## 与参考绑定
优先使用局部特写图作为 <Subject N> 或 <Picture N>，retention 写 fully_preserved for genital anatomy。

## 只有穿衣服图或纯文生时

- retention 用条件句覆盖：`When lower body is revealed: correctly proportioned [male/female] genitals remain fully present and accurately shaped.`
- 男娘场景额外强调 “clearly male” 与 “do not replace with female genitals”。
