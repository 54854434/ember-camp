# 液体、湿润与高潮表现

## 原则
- 用具体、可见的描述，避免抽象或过度脏乱词。
- 液体要有来源、质感、运动方式。
- 与动作同步，不要单独飘着。

## 常用表达

### 爱液 / 湿润
- `her genitals are visibly wet and glistening`
- `clear fluid coats the shaft as it moves in and out`
- `wet sounds of skin and fluid synchronized with each thrust`

### 精液
- `thick white semen`
- `he cums, shooting thick ropes of semen onto her skin / into her / across her breasts`
- `semen drips slowly down her skin with natural gravity`
- 避免瞬间爆炸式或过多粒子。

### 口水 / 口交液体
- `saliva glistens on the shaft and drips from her lower lip`
- `wet sucking sounds`

### 潮吹（谨慎使用）
- `a sudden spray of clear fluid as she reaches climax`
- 仅在用户明确要求时使用，并控制量与方向。

### 高潮身体反应
- `her body tenses and trembles`
- `her inner walls contract around him`
- `she arches her back, toes curling, soft continuous moans rising in pitch`
- 与声音、表情同步。
